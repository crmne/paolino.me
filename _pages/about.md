I'm Carmine Paolino, a software engineer, founder, and musician in Berlin. I build products, frameworks, and open-source tools across AI, Ruby, and native desktop software. I run [Plenty][plenty], the company behind this work.

I created [RubyLLM][rubyllm], a framework for building AI applications in Ruby, and build [Chat with Work][chatwithwork], an AI assistant that connects to your work tools. My developer tools include [ArchSpec][archspec] for executable architecture rules, [Schematist][schematist] for JSON Schema, and [Kamal Backup][kamalbackup] for database and file backups.

I also build native desktop apps: [Spotifast][spotifast], a Spotify client, and [ZapFast][zapfast], a WhatsApp client. On Linux, I work on [hyprmoncfg][hyprmoncfg] and [Omarchy plugins][omarchyplugins]. My [project directory][projects] collects what I build and maintain.

I build the software I need and make it a pleasure to use. I care about speed, beauty, and the details that make a tool feel right, from the interface down to the code.

I help organize [Berlin.rb][berlinrb], a monthly meetup for Berlin's Ruby community. I founded [Floppy Disco][floppydisco], a Berlin music collective, and make house and disco as [Crimson Lake][crimsonlake].

I wrote my first scripts at 5 to automate launching my favorite games. I've been hooked ever since.

I got into open source early, running the Italian [Gentoo Linux][gentoo] community as a teenager. Studied CS at the [University of Bologna][unibo], where I published work on [large-scale social network analysis][lana] with [Springer][springer]. Then AI at the [Free University of Amsterdam][vu], combining deep learning with music. Worked at [OLX][olx], [Capgemini][capgemini], and the [Alan Turing Institute][turing].

Before Plenty, I co-founded [Freshflow][freshflow]: AI that tells supermarkets how much fresh produce to order so less of it ends up in the bin.
