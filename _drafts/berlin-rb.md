Berlin has one of the largest Ruby communities in Europe and not enough places to meet.

So a few of us started one. [Berlin.rb](https://berlinrb.org) is a new monthly meetup for people who write Ruby, or are curious about it. Talks, project show-and-tells, and time with other people who work in the same language. It's free and open to anyone, whether you've been writing Ruby for fifteen years or installed it last week.

**The first one is Tuesday, September 8, 18:30, at [ablefy](https://ablefy.io) on Potsdamer Straße.** After that, the second Tuesday of every month.

[Register here.](https://luma.com/lnmj7085) If you can't make it to Berlin, there's a [livestream](https://luma.com/psvh08jx) and it'll be on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@berlinrb_org) afterwards.

## Why another one

Berlin already has a long-running Ruby User Group, and it's good. This isn't a replacement for it and it isn't a reaction to it.

It's about cadence. Berlin has enough Rubyists to fill a room every month, and right now there's no monthly Ruby meetup in the city. We wanted one you can rely on: second Tuesday, every month, same rhythm, so you put it in your calendar once and stop checking.

## Bigger than Berlin

We don't want this to be just a room in Berlin.

Berlin.rb is part of [Ruby Europe](https://rubyeurope.com), the umbrella bringing together Ruby communities across the continent and moving speakers between cities. A talk that works in Berlin should be able to travel to Warsaw or Lisbon, and theirs should come here.

We're also teaming up with **Ruby Connect**, Chikahiro Tokoro's project for connecting Japanese Rubyists with the rest of the world. Ruby was born in Japan and a huge number of the people who build it are still there, largely in conversation with each other rather than with the rest of us. That gap is one of the strangest things about our language, and closing it is worth real effort. We want Berlin to be one of the places it closes.

## Come talk

We want speakers, and we're not precious about it. A deep technical talk, a lightning talk about something you built last weekend, a story about something that went badly in production. If you've never given a talk before, this is a good place for the first one.

Send a sentence or two to [hello@berlinrb.org](mailto:hello@berlinrb.org) and we'll take it from there.

## Host or sponsor us

We want to move around the city. Different neighbourhoods, different offices, so it's easy to reach wherever you happen to live, and so you get to see where other Berlin Ruby teams actually work.

If your company has a space free on a weekday evening, or wants to cover food and drinks, we'd love to hear from you. Sponsorship is what keeps this free and open to everyone, and hosting is the easiest way to put your team in front of Berlin's Ruby community.
